Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Overview

Physio MCQ ("we", "our", "the site") is a free practice tool for Physiotherapy students. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the site at neetikacodes.github.io/physio-mcq, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

Information We Collect

Physio MCQ does not require account creation, sign-up, or login. We do not knowingly collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information through normal use of the quiz.

  • Local storage: your most recent quiz score summary (subject, topic, correct count, percentage) is saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. This information is never transmitted to us or any third party — it simply lets your browser show you a recap next time you visit.
  • Report Issue form: if you choose to use the "Report Issue" button, the details you type are sent to GitHub (as a pre-filled GitHub Issue) when you submit — this is voluntary and only happens if you actively use that feature.

Cookies & Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

If analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) are enabled on this site in the future, they may collect standard, non-personally-identifying usage data (such as pages visited, approximate location, device/browser type, and time spent) to help us understand how the site is used and improve it.

Third-Party Links

This site may link out to third-party services (for example, GitHub, for issue reporting). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of any linked external site. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit.

Children's Privacy

Physio MCQ is intended for physiotherapy students and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
