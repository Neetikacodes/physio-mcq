About Physio MCQ

Physio MCQ is a free practice tool built for Physiotherapy students preparing for MPT, AIIMS, and Railway Physiotherapy exams. Questions are organized by subject, topic, and difficulty so you can focus your revision exactly where you need it.

Please note: this platform is intended for practice and self-assessment only. Use it to test your knowledge and identify weak areas — not as a substitute for your official textbooks, syllabus, or coursework.
